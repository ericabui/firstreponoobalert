## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the warmth, craft, and convivial spirit of an artisanal Neapolitan pizzeria translated into a contemporary digital dining experience. The visual language blends rustic heritage with modern clarity: the fiery warmth of a wood-fired oven meets the crisp, effortless utility of a bespoke culinary ordering interface.

The design movement is **Warm Modern Editorial with Tactile Depth**. It eschews sterile tech minimalism in favor of rich, appetizing warmth—pairing classical typography and sun-drenched Italian color palettes with soft, physical-inspired components (sunken counters, pill controls, floating cart bars). The resulting UI feels welcoming, grounded, and intensely appetizing, building anticipation from the first menu scroll to the final checkout step.

## Colors

The palette draws directly from the artisanal pizza-making tradition: glowing wood embers, golden sourdough crusts, fresh sweet basil, and stone-milled flour.

- **Primary (`#C83E29` - Terracotta Brick)**: The dominant brand tone used for high-impact actions, active selections, price emphasis, and key interactive focal points.
- **Secondary (`#E59838` - Crust Amber)**: Accent tone for appetizing callouts, chef’s specials, ratings, crust selections, and secondary badges.
- **Tertiary (`#386641` - Basil Olive)**: Functional and culinary accent for dietary tags (vegetarian, organic, DOP-certified), confirmation states, and fresh ingredient callouts.
- **Neutral Dark (`#231F20` - Espresso Roast)**: High-contrast, rich charcoal replacement for body text, strong titles, and deep iconography. Never use pure black `#000000`.
- **Canvas (`#FDFBF7` - Flour White)**: The default warm, porous canvas background that provides soft light without harsh digital glare.
- **Surface (`#F4EFE6` - Warm Stone)**: Secondary surface layer for inset containers, table rows, steppers, and pill track backgrounds.
- **Card Background (`#FFFFFF` - Crisp Mozzarella)**: Elevated card containers that float above the flour canvas to spotlight menu items and order modules.
- **Borders & Dividers (`#EADFD5` - Muted Tuscan Mortar)**: Subtle structural definition that preserves spatial hierarchy without cutting into the warm atmosphere.

## Typography

The typography pairing contrasts an Italian heritage serif with a crisp, geometric sans-serif:

1. **Headlines (`Playfair Display`)**: Conveys culinary distinction, artisanal tradition, and rustic elegance. Reserved for hero statements, menu category titles, pizza names, and modal headlines. Never used for functional tabular data or microcopy.
2. **Body, Interface & Numerics (`Plus Jakarta Sans`)**: Provides immediate scanning clarity across menu item descriptions, calorie notes, customization options, ingredient checklists, and prices. The open counters and geometric forms guarantee legibility on both mobile screens in low-lit dining rooms and desktop displays.
3. **Numerics & Prices**: Set in `Plus Jakarta Sans` bold to anchor pricing with modern precision, avoiding any ambiguity during the ordering and receipt flow.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout follows a fluid-responsive grid system designed around food photography, customizable item cards, and rapid ordering ergonomics:

- **Desktop (1024px+)**: 12-column grid, max canvas width of 1280px, with `gutter: 1.5rem` and `margin: 2rem`. Allows a 2-column menu layout alongside a persistent 4-column sticky order receipt sidebar.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px)**: 8-column grid with `gutter: 1.25rem` and `margin: 1.5rem`. Menu items reflow into a clean 2-column card stack; cart collapses into a floating bottom pill.
- **Mobile (320px - 767px)**: 4-column fluid layout with `gutter-mobile: 1rem` and `margin-mobile: 1rem`. Item cards collapse into single-column vertical cards or horizontal split cards (thumbnail on left, title and price stepper on right). 
- **Bottom Clearance**: On mobile screens, all main content containers must implement a mandatory bottom padding of `5.5rem` to prevent UI occlusion caused by the sticky bottom checkout bar.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system uses warm, tinted ambient shadows rather than cold industrial dropshadows. Shadows carry a hint of deep terracotta espresso (`#231F20` mixed with `#C83E29` undertones at low opacity) to recreate the diffused glow of ambient restaurant lighting.

- **Level 0 (Flat Canvas)**: Background canvas (`#FDFBF7`) and inset container wells (`#F4EFE6`). Insets use a 1px border (`#EADFD5`) with zero shadow.
- **Level 1 (Resting Cards & Steppers)**: White cards (`#FFFFFF`) sitting over the canvas. Box shadow: `0 2px 8px -2px rgba(35, 31, 32, 0.05), 0 1px 3px rgba(35, 31, 32, 0.03)` with a hairline border (`#EADFD5`).
- **Level 2 (Hover Cards & Active Pills)**: Interactive food cards under pointer focus and selected state items. Box shadow: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(35, 31, 32, 0.08), 0 3px 6px -2px rgba(35, 31, 32, 0.04)`.
- **Level 3 (Sticky Checkout Bar & Modals)**: Floating order capsules, customizer drawers, and sticky checkout summaries. Box shadow: `0 12px 36px -4px rgba(200, 62, 41, 0.14), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(35, 31, 32, 0.08)`.

## Shapes

The roundedness level is set to `2` (Base `0.5rem` / 8px). 

- **Cards & Surfaces (`rounded-lg` / 1rem - 16px)**: Applied to menu cards, modal sheets, and customization containers, reflecting the organic, hand-stretched contours of pizza dough without feeling overly cartoonish.
- **Pills & Selectors (`rounded-full` / 9999px)**: Reserved for high-touch, tactile UI controls including pizza size options (10" / 12" / 16"), dietary badges, and the bottom checkout pill.
- **Interactive Steppers (`rounded-lg` / 0.5rem - 8px)**: Compact quantity counters and modifier toggles.
- **Hero Image Masks (`rounded-xl` / 1.5rem - 24px)**: Photography containers showcase rounded rustic framing that anchors the artisanal aesthetic.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action**: Solid Terracotta Brick (`#C83E29`) with flour white text (`#FDFBF7`), `rounded-full` shape, padding `0.875rem 1.75rem`, font `label-lg`. Subtle hover lift with a darkened ember shift (`#B23320`).
- **Secondary / Outline**: Muted border (`1.5px solid #EADFD5`), surface `#FFFFFF`, text `#231F20`. On hover: surface transitions to `#F4EFE6` with border `#C83E29`.
- **Accent Button**: Warm Crust Amber (`#E59838`) background with rich espresso text (`#231F20`) for special add-on promotions and upsell moments.

### Pill-Shaped Size Selectors
- Displayed as a segmented horizontal pill track with background `#F4EFE6` and `p-1`.
- **Unselected State**: Transparent background, text `#231F20`, font `label-md`.
- **Selected State**: High-contrast white (`#FFFFFF`) or Terracotta (`#C83E29`) pill, `rounded-full`, elevation Level 1, bold text. Clearly highlights diameter (e.g., `12" Regular`) alongside dynamic price differentials (`+$3.50`).

### Quantity Steppers (+/- Counters)
- Enclosed pill or rounded modular container (`#F4EFE6`).
- Left decrement (`-`) and right increment (`+`) touch targets minimum 36x36px with rounded circular feedback.
- Numeric value centered in `price-display` or `title-md` font.
- Decrement morphs into a subtle trash-can icon when quantity equals 1.

### Sticky Bottom Checkout Summary Pill
- Floats centered at the bottom of the viewport with a fixed max-width (540px on desktop/tablet, `calc(100% - 2rem)` on mobile).
- Dark espresso base (`#231F20`) or primary terracotta (`#C83E29`) with Level 3 elevation.
- **Interior Layout**: Left side displays item count badge (e.g., "3 Pizzas") and subtotal; right side hosts the checkout call-to-action with an arrow icon.

### Segmented Order Receipt List
- Clean receipt-style card with `#FFFFFF` background, `#EADFD5` border, and Level 1 elevation.
- Items separated by clean 1px hairline dividers.
- Line items feature the item name in `headline-sm`, customized modifiers (e.g., "Extra Buffalo Mozzarella", "Charred Crust") in `body-sm` muted text, and tabular right-aligned price.
- Bottom totals section utilizes a distinct dashed or warm stone (`#F4EFE6`) summary block.

### Badges & Dietary Tags
- **Artisanal / Chef Pick**: Background `#FDF3E7`, text `#E59838`, subtle border `#F7DCB4`.
- **DOP / Fresh Basil / Vegetarian**: Background `#EAF2EC`, text `#386641`, border `#C6DEC9`.
- **Spicy / Wood-Fired Special**: Background `#FAECE9`, text `#C83E29`, border `#F1C6BF`.
- Shape: `rounded-full`, padding `0.25rem 0.625rem`, typography `label-sm`, tracking uppercase.

### Input Fields & Special Instructions
- Background `#FFFFFF`, 1.5px border `#EADFD5`, `rounded-lg` (8px), padding `0.75rem 1rem`.
- Focus state: Border color transitions smoothly to `#C83E29` with a subtle 3px glow ring `rgba(200, 62, 41, 0.15)`. Placeholder text in warm muted gray (`#8C827A`).
